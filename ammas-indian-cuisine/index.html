Our story
"Every dish is served with love, tradition, and a little taste of home."

Amma means mother, and our menu is built around the food we grew up eating. Andhra classics like Konaseema kodi vepudu, Vijayawada biryani and Nellore fish pulusu sit next to the curries and Indo-Chinese favourites everyone orders.

We opened Amma's so you could eat the food we grew up with: generous, properly spiced and served the way it is at home. Come hungry.
